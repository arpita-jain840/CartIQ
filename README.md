# 🛍️ CartIQ – Intelligent Shopping Assistant

### Shop Smart. Choose Intelligently.

CartIQ is an ML-powered shopping assistant designed to help users make smarter purchasing decisions through personalized product recommendations, product comparisons, and intelligent product analysis.

Instead of overwhelming customers with thousands of product choices, CartIQ focuses on understanding their needs, preferences, and budget to recommend products that best match their requirements.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Online shopping platforms offer a huge variety of products, making it difficult for customers to identify the most suitable option according to their budget, requirements, and preferences.

Users often spend considerable time comparing product specifications, prices, ratings, and features before making a purchase decision.

CartIQ aims to simplify this process by using Machine Learning to provide personalized, explainable, and data-driven product recommendations.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

CartIQ acts as a personal shopping intelligence system that:

- Understands user requirements and shopping preferences.
- Recommends suitable products based on individual needs.
- Compares products based on price, ratings, and specifications.
- Explains why a particular product is recommended.
- Identifies similar products and better alternatives.
- Learns from user interactions to improve future recommendations.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Smart Product Recommendations:** Personalized product suggestions using Machine Learning.
- **Product Comparison:** Compare products based on important features and specifications.
- **Explainable Recommendations:** Understand why a product matches your requirements.
- **Value-for-Money Analysis:** Identify products offering better value according to user preferences.
- **Similar Product Discovery:** Find alternatives with similar features.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Group customers based on shopping behaviour.

## 🤖 Machine Learning Approach

CartIQ will explore the following Machine Learning techniques:

| Algorithm / Technique | Application |
|---|---|
| K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) | Finding similar products |
| Cosine Similarity | Measuring product similarity |
| K-Means Clustering | Customer segmentation |
| TF-IDF | Processing product descriptions |
| Random Forest | Predicting customer preferences |

## 🌟 What Makes CartIQ Different?

Unlike basic product recommendation systems, CartIQ aims to focus on the customer's actual requirements rather than simply recommending popular products.

Its key objective is to make recommendations more personalized, explainable, and useful for real-world purchasing decisions.
